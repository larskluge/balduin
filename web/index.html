Balduin der Ball

A 16-bit DOS platformer from 2004, written in x86 assembly — running in your browser via js-dos.

Controls

  • ← / → — move left / right
  • ↑ — jump
  • Esc — quit

Goal

  • Collect every diamond on a level to advance to the next one.
  • Water, deep water, and spikes kill Balduin; the level restarts.
  • The world wraps horizontally — walk off one edge, come back on the other.
Emulation by js-dos (DOSBox compiled to WebAssembly). Click inside the screen first if the keyboard isn’t responding.
